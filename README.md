# corporate_website_modernization
Corporate Website Modernization: Enhancing Cross-Platform UX and Digital Branding for Enterprise Growth

The first issues why we should change our official website are:
- The website currently not figured our company as current situation, the website was built in 2020 which our business is small we only have 3 branches but at that time we have already 8 branches and still continue build new branches.
- Currently many people access internet through smartphone which is hard to read our website use smartphone. So we build website which compatible with any platforms
- Difficult to maintain content because our team don't know the password for changing content

To solved above problem we decided to collect all information about company which should published to others people.
We use SWOT (Strengths Weaknesses Opportunities Threats) methodology
Strengths:
- Our distributor was established since 1972 (54 years old)
- We are sold agent of One biggest Pharmaceutical in Indonesia
- We are lead company in medical device and orthopedic solution
Weaknesses:
- Our branches are less than others pharmaceutical distributors
- Some of our branches domicile in Java only one branch which out Java's island
- We arose become national distributors since Indonesia's economy crisis in 1997
Opportunities:
- Even though our branches are only 8, we have distributor partner which established Indonesia's cities
- All our branches are has own building
- We start to build a new warehouse which has capacity larger than before
Threats:
- Government Regulation, we should prepare any possibility which make impact to our business.
- Rapid Technology growth, we should give solution to our customer which new experience
- High turnover employees in our company

Conclusion:
1. We build the responsive web design which can be opened in several devices like laptop, desktop computer, tablet and mobile phone
2. Show the journey or milestone company since our company established until current era.
3. Show position our all own branches and partner branches in Indonesia Map
